\begin{frame}{What is AWS SageMaker HyperPod?}
\small
Managed, \textbf{resilient GPU/Trainium clusters} for large-scale foundation-model work --- one
cluster spanning \textbf{training} and \textbf{inference}, with self-healing hardware that
survives accelerator failures.

\vfill
\centering
\scalebox{1.0}{\begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=0.5cm and 1.1cm, font=\scriptsize,
                    every node/.style={align=center}]
  % --- left: dev + orchestration ---
  \node[box] (dev) {Recipes \& dev\\JupyterLab / VSCode\\HyperPod recipes};
  \node[box, below=of dev] (orch) {Orchestrate (pick one)\\\textbf{Slurm} \; $|$ \; \textbf{Amazon EKS}};

  % --- center: the cluster group ---
  \node[box, right=of dev]                 (w1) {GPU / Trainium\\worker node};
  \node[box, right=0.3cm of w1]            (w2) {worker node\\$\cdots$ thousands};
  \node[box, below=of w1]                  (head) {head / login\\node};
  \node[hl,  right=0.3cm of head]          (res) {Resiliency agent\\deep health checks\\auto-replace $<$2\,min};
  \begin{scope}[on background layer]
    \node[grp, fit=(w1)(w2)(head)(res), label={[font=\scriptsize\bfseries]above:SageMaker HyperPod cluster (your VPC)}] (cluster) {};
  \end{scope}

  % --- right: the two workloads on the same cluster ---
  \node[sbx, right=of w2]                  (train) {\textbf{Training \& fine-tuning}\\LLMs · diffusion · FMs\\up to 95\% goodput};
  \node[sbx, below=of train]               (infer) {\textbf{Production inference}\\tiered KV cache\\auto-recovery};

  % --- bottom band: shared services (compact) ---
  \node[box, font=\tiny, minimum height=0.5cm, below=0.6cm of cluster.south, anchor=north] (obs) {Observability: MLflow / Grafana / CloudWatch};
  \node[box, font=\tiny, minimum height=0.5cm, left=0.3cm of obs]  (fsx) {FSx for Lustre storage};
  \node[box, font=\tiny, minimum height=0.5cm, right=0.3cm of obs] (gov) {Task Governance ($\downarrow$cost)};

  % --- arrows: inputs enter the cluster (left), workloads run on it (right) ---
  \draw[ar] (dev.east)  -- (cluster.west |- dev);   % recipes/dev submit into the cluster (top-left)
  \draw[ar] (orch.east) -- (cluster.west |- orch);  % orchestrator drives the cluster (bottom-left)
  \coordinate (wl) at ([xshift=0.45cm]cluster.east);
  \draw[thick] (cluster.east) -- (wl);              % single stub off the cluster's right edge
  \draw[ar] (wl) |- (train.west);                   % cluster runs training
  \draw[ar] (wl) |- (infer.west);                   % cluster runs inference
  \draw[arb] (cluster.south) -- (obs.north);        % cluster <-> shared services (storage / observability)
\end{tikzpicture}}

\vfill
\raggedright\footnotesize
\textbf{In one line:} provision a cluster once; HyperPod keeps the accelerators healthy while you
train, fine-tune, and serve foundation models on it.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{SageMaker HyperPod --- key features \& example workflows}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[t]
  \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
    \textbf{Key features}
    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{5pt}
      \item \textbf{Self-healing resiliency:} deep health checks detect \& swap faulty nodes in
            $<$2\,min --- checkpointless, no restart cycles; up to \textbf{95\% training goodput}.
      \item \textbf{Orchestrator choice:} Slurm \emph{and} Amazon EKS on the \emph{same} cluster;
            also Ray and topology-aware scheduling.
      \item \textbf{Efficient scale:} thousands of NVIDIA H100/A100 or AWS Trainium; UltraServer
            \emph{NVL72} = 72 Blackwell GPUs over NVLink.
      \item \textbf{Cost \& tooling:} up to \textbf{40\%} (Task Governance) / \textbf{65\%} (Flexible
            Training Plans) savings, MIG fractional GPUs; FSx for Lustre, recipes \& observability.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{column}
  \begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
    \textbf{Example workflows}
    \begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{4pt}
      \item \textbf{Pre-train} a 100B+ LLM across thousands of H100s --- survive GPU failures
            without losing days of progress.
      \item \textbf{Fine-tune} an open model (Llama / diffusion) via a HyperPod recipe with
            job auto-resume on EKS.
      \item \textbf{Serve} the trained model on the \emph{same} cluster --- up to \textbf{25\%
            cheaper} and \textbf{40\% lower latency} with tiered KV cache.
      \item \textbf{Mixed teams:} Task Governance shares one cluster across training and inference
            jobs with priority-based allocation.
    \end{itemize}

    \vspace{4pt}
    \textbf{In production:} Perplexity, Luma AI, Stability AI.
  \end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}
